About TYT

Coffee is the excuse.
Taking your time is the point.

TYT was built on a simple idea: a good cup deserves a good pause. This is a coffee lounge where the seats are comfortable on purpose, the pace is unhurried on purpose, and the menu covers whatever the moment calls for — a fast espresso, a slow specialty pour, or a shake to share.

Whether you're catching up with friends, getting through a stack of work, or just want somewhere warm to sit with a good drink, TYT is built around one house rule: take your time.

"Take a break. Take your time."
